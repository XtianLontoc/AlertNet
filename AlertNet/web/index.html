Waiting for the first reading

Live values appear here as soon as your ESP32 starts sending readings. Until then, the cards below stay empty. This page updates by itself. Optionally, the ESP32 can also send wifi_rssi (signal strength in dBm) and uptime_s (seconds since it booted) to fill in the Device health panel.

  1. Power the ESP32 and connect it to Wi-Fi.
  2. Have it send the JSON shown here every few seconds.
  3. Send it to the address shown here with a POST request. If an access key is set, add it as an X-API-Key header.

Safety limits

                
            

            

                

                    
                    
                    A reading at or above this temperature is reported as a hazard. Allowed range: 30 to 150.
                

                

                    
                    
                    A gas reading at or above this level is reported as a hazard. Raw values depend on your sensor and wiring, so adjust after testing. Allowed range: 50 to 10000.
                

                

                    
                    
                

                
These limits drive the status bar, the cards and the chart limit lines. If your ESP32 decides hazards in its own code, update the limits there too.
